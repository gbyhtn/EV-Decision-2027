# Project Principles

## Mission

Provide an open, transparent and evidence-based framework for evaluating battery electric vehicles.

---

## Principle 1

Facts and opinions are stored separately.

---

## Principle 2

Every subjective score requires documentation.

---

## Principle 3

Every score has a confidence value.

---

## Principle 4

OTA updates may improve or reduce ratings.

---

## Principle 5

KO criteria override the total score.

---

## Principle 6

The project is manufacturer independent.

---

## Principle 7

Every evaluation is reproducible.

---

## Principle 8

All weighting is user configurable.
